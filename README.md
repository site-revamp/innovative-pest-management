# Innovative Pest — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Innovative Pest Pte. Ltd. provides residential, commercial and specialist pest management services in Singapore. Its Integrated Pest Management approach combines inspection, pest identification, targeted treatment and ongoing monitoring.

The proposed homepage helps homeowners, businesses and property managers find relevant services while giving SmartIPM technology a clearer role in the company's positioning.

## Main Improvements

1. **Clearer hierarchy and service navigation**  
   Give the main proposition more space and prioritise residential and commercial entry points. Introduce specialist pest services progressively, reducing competition between statistics, promotional messages and pest categories.

2. **More visual explanation of SmartIPM**  
   Create a dedicated section explaining monitoring, reporting and targeted treatment through concise copy, diagrams and authentic equipment photographs. This makes the technology-supported approach easier to understand than long technical descriptions. Visuals should reflect confirmed capabilities.

3. **Consistent trust information and focused enquiries**  
   Standardise confirmed phone and WhatsApp details, distinguish the company-stated 2015 founding from its 2017 incorporation, and present verified credentials clearly. Simplify inspection enquiries with separate prompts for residential customers and commercial decision-makers. Confirm free-inspection eligibility before presenting it as an offer.

## Design Improvements

- Establish a clearer sequence from the opening proposition to service selection, SmartIPM, credentials and enquiries.
- Use consistent headings and service layouts to reduce visual density.
- Give the technology explanation and relevant enquiry actions greater visual emphasis.

## Implementation Status

This summary documents the proposed redesign direction. Implementation has not been verified against a redesigned homepage draft. Contact details, credentials and inspection terms require confirmation before launch.
